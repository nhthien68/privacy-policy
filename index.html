Official App Homepage

CanLua

CanLua is a mobile application for recording rice-weighing sessions, calculating received weight and payment amounts, and exporting session data to Excel. Your main weighing data is stored locally on your device.

What CanLua does

Tools for managing rice-weighing records from a mobile device.

Record weighing sessions

Enter bag weights and keep session details, notes, rice variety, trader information, and weighing records together.

Calculate totals

Calculate received weight, bag totals, prices, deposits, paid amounts, handling fees, and payment status.

Export and back up

Export a weighing session as an Excel file, or manually back up and restore app data through Google Drive.

Google Sign-In & Data Usage

Google access is used only when you choose to back up or restore data.

User initiated

Why CanLua uses Google Sign-In

CanLua uses Google Sign-In to let you select and authorize your Google account for the optional Google Drive backup and restore feature. You can use CanLua's core weighing features without signing in to Google.

Google Drive data requested

When you start a backup or restore, CanLua uses the Google Drive API to create or read canlua-backup.json in the private appDataFolder area for CanLua. The backup may contain weighing sessions, weighing tables, individual weight entries, and app settings.

CanLua requests access only to its app-specific Drive data area. It does not read all files in your Google Drive, and it does not access Gmail or Google Calendar. Backup and restore occur only after you request them in the app.
